# Casava products and site features

## Company tagline
Casava's public site positions the brand as "Insurance you enjoy" - cover for the things people love, at prices they describe as less than the cost of a taxi ride.

## Health Insurance
"Insure my health" - affordable healthcare plans so people can use hospitals without breaking the bank. Customer stories on the site mention easy hospital visits with a Casava policy number and low monthly contributions on some plans.

## Health Cash
"Get health cash" - cash back for hospital visits. This sits alongside health insurance as a separate product on casava.com.

## Business Gro
For SMEs: business insurance (fire and flood), training, and loan access. See business-gro.md for full detail. Coverage for this briefing is up to ₦1,000,000.

## Other products mentioned publicly
Casava's privacy materials list services including health insurance, health cash, Business Gro, and credit life products. The about page also references work on income protection concepts for workers.

## Signup experience
Casava markets signup as taking about 90 seconds, with long forms removed, and often promotes a one-month free insurance trial on health-oriented offers. Exact promo terms change - check the live site when buying.

## Claims speed (general)
Casava states claims are hassle-free, digital, and paid within 48 hours of an approved application for the products that use that process.

## Digital tools
Casava talks about digital products (including Bloom in public writing) for renewing premiums, filing claims, and receiving payments on a phone.

## Who Casava serves
Individuals seeking health cover and health cash, plus small business owners seeking Business Gro. The company reports 95k+ customers and strategic partners on its about page.
