# About Casava

## Who they are
Casava (Casava Microinsurance Limited) is a general and life microinsurance company licensed by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM), registered in Nigeria, and built for insurance in Africa.

## Mission
They aim to simplify protection with a delightful user experience, strong value, and instant benefits so people can thrive and prosper. Public messaging: "We are making Insurance Relevant."

## Name meaning
Casava is a play on cassava - a staple crop in tropical regions. Nigeria is a major cassava producer. The brand links that idea to nourishment and prosperity for African and emerging communities.

## Scale (public stats)
On casava.com/about they highlight roughly 95k+ customers, 5 strategic partners, and $16M in insurance coverage figures as published on that page.

## Leadership note
The homepage has featured founder Bode Pedro (e.g. C-Suite Insider CEO of the Year recognition). Useful only as company context, not for product decisions.

## Regulation
Licensed by NAICOM. Not a bank; insurance products and claims follow policy terms and Nigerian insurance regulation.
