# Claims and support

## General Casava claims
Casava promotes a digital claims process. Many products: use File Claim in your Casava account, or visit https://claims.casava.co and follow the steps. They often say you can expect a response within 24 hours, and approved claims paid within 48 hours.

## Business Gro / SMEDAN-linked claims
For Business Gro insurance claims, notify via email to info@smedanregister.ng with:
- Photos of the incident
- Cost breakdown of affected items

Keep copies of everything you send.

## Exclusions
Policy exclusions are in the document you get after first premium payment. Do not assume a loss is covered until that document (or support) confirms it.

## Changing hospitals (health products)
For health plans, Casava materials describe managing hospital choices and claims through the Casava account. That flow is for health products, not Business Gro shop insurance.
