# Business Gro Insurance

## What Business Gro is
Business Gro is Casava's offer for small businesses in Nigeria. It bundles business insurance (fire and flood), training, and access to business loans so shop owners can keep going when something unexpected hits.

## Coverage limit
For this product briefing, Business Gro Insurance provides up to ₦1,000,000 (one million naira) in coverage for eligible small businesses. Exact limits and exclusions are confirmed in the policy document you receive after your first premium payment.

## What the insurance covers
Business Gro business insurance focuses on fire and flood damage that can interrupt a shop or small business. It is meant for business continuity and financial protection when natural or unexpected events hit your premises or stock.

## Cost / premium
Public Casava materials describe Business Gro at a small monthly fee commonly stated as ₦200 (two hundred naira). Payment can be made monthly, quarterly, or annually. Confirm the live price on SMEDAN / Casava before you pay.

## Who qualifies (eligibility)
Small and medium entrepreneurs with businesses registered on the SMEDAN platform are eligible. If your bakery or shop is not yet on SMEDAN, register there first - Business Gro is tied to that membership.

## How to apply / buy
1. Register as a member on SMEDAN.
2. Once registration is complete, Business Gro shows up on your SMEDAN dashboard.
3. Activate and pay the small monthly fee to unlock insurance, training, and loan access.

Payment options mentioned in Casava's help materials include USSD on MTN: dial *6555# and follow the prompts (you may get SMS with next steps).

## How to make a claim (Business Gro)
To start a Business Gro claim, email info@smedanregister.ng. Attach:
- Photographic evidence of the incident
- A breakdown of the cost of the items affected

Claims handling begins once those materials are received. For other Casava products you can also use claims.casava.co or File Claim inside your Casava account (response often within 24 hours). Approved claims messaging on Casava's site includes payouts within 48 hours of an approved application for many products - always follow the instructions for your specific policy.

## What is not covered / exclusions
Exclusions live in your full policy document. After the first premium payment you receive that document. If you are unsure whether a bakery-specific risk is covered, ask Casava support before you assume it is.

## Training and loans
Besides insurance, Business Gro includes professional training / educational content and tools for growth, plus pathways toward business loans for scaling.
